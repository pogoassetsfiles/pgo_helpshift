**Issue Description:**
For certain 5km and 10km eggs, the egg will incorrectly display the list of 2km Pokémon.
This a visual bug only, the Pokémon hatching from the 10km egg will still be from the correct grouping for that egg's km.

**Issue Status:**
Investigating - to be fixed in a future update.
