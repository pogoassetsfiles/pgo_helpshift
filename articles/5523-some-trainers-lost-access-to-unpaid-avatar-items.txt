title: Some Trainers lost access to unpaid avatar items

**Issue description:** Following a recent mitigation for an unrelated issue, a small subset of Trainers were temporarily able to equip premium avatar items without paying PokéCoins. These items were not granted to player inventories. Once a permanent fix was deployed, any unpaid avatar items were automatically unequipped.

**Issue status:**  Resolved / Working as Intended. No paid items or legitimately acquired inventory were affected.
