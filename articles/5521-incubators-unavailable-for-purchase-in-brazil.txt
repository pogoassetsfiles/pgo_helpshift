title: Incubators unavailable for purchase in Brazil

**Issue Description:** In Brazil, paid Incubators and Super Incubators are unavailable for individual purchase. This change was made to ensure the game remains broadly accessible to players under local requirements. Bundles, Event Tickets, GO Pass Deluxe upgrades, and other offers or reward paths that include Incubators or Super Incubators are also unavailable. Offers that do not include those items will remain available. Specific offers may vary by event. Incubators can still be earned through gameplay.
 **Issue Status:** Working as intended as we investigate long-term solutions.
